THE SYSTEM FAILURE

Why 62 Million Tons of Electronics are Dumped Annually

Planned Fast-Obsolescence: Modern consumer electronics are engineered with glued glass sandwiches, soldered storage, and proprietary serialized parts that display bogus error codes when swapped.

Information Asymmetry: Consumers don't know whether a device needs a simple $15 battery swap or a $400 motherboard replacement. In doubt, 78% simply buy new.

Data Panic: Fear of personal photos, bank logins, and emails lingering on memory chips causes functional devices to sit idle in drawers for years before ending up in landfills.

Our Trust and Transparency Commitments

CIRCULAR operates with full algorithmic neutrality. We do not accept advertising kickbacks from device manufacturers to steer people toward buying new hardware.

1

Repair First, Always

We never suggest purchasing a replacement if a device has an economical, viable repair or component upgrade pathway.

Built for the Right-to-Repair Era

Aligned directly with the 2024 EU Right to Repair Directive, US State Right to Repair acts, and India’s LiFE circular economy mission. We remove the consumer friction point by turning complex teardown specs into executable triage.

PROSPECTIVE PARTNER ECOSYSTEM

Honest Prospective Integrations

No fake endorsements. Our deployment model targets integration with open APIs from iFixit for teardown steps, Repair.org for shop vetting, and certified R2v3 / e-Stewards logistics handlers for responsible scrap smelting.

DATA-BACKED VERIFICATION

Cryptographic Impact Telemetry

Every completed repair logs a SHA-256 hashed hardware serial token and receipt record. This avoids double-counting and creates verifiable carbon avoidance credits for municipal and corporate sustainability audits.
