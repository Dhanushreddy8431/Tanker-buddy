Smart Sump Auto-Refill: Zero Dry Taps, Zero Burnt Pumps

Most Bangalore residents wake up to dry taps when underground sumps run empty unnoticed at 3:00 AM, burning high-stage booster pumps. Tanker Buddy mounts an ultrasonic non-contact sensor inside your sump that automatically cues tanker dispatch before you hit critical reserve.

Non-Contact Ultrasonic Sensing (IP68)

Mounts magnetically or bracketed above water line. Zero rust, zero mineral fouling, 3-year sealed lithium cell.
